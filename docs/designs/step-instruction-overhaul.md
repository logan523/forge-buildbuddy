## Vision

### 10x Check
A complete beginner opens step 6 on their phone at the kitchen table. The 3D stage frames exactly the two pins involved; the wire in the text, the 2D technique drawing, and the 3D scene are all the same blue because they share one color authority. A Connections table lists both ends of every wire — pins named by their silkscreen labels, rendered from the plan's netlist so prose can never disagree with it. "Check your work" says what the multimeter should read. Real bench photos from the founder's own build sit under the hero. If they're confused, they type a question and the answer quotes the same compiled facts; on the demo they can photograph their board for an abstain-biased AI check. It feels like a senior engineer at the workbench.

**Honest framing (rev 3):** compiled facts guarantee CONSISTENCY — text, diagram, and 3D cannot disagree — not physical correctness. For generated plans the netlist itself comes from the LLM (checked by ERC for internal consistency); the demo's netlist is hand-verified. Grades are "consistent"/"derived", never "verified".

### Why now
Audit found: steps are generated by ONE Claude call with one specificity constraint and zero post-generation validation, while the netlist/firmware/parts/wire-color ground truth sits unused; even the hand-tuned demo contradicts itself (SDA "green" vs "blue"). The 2D/3D hierarchy flipped twice (1331a43 → working tree): the split is the defect.

### Core vs expansion
D1 (compiler + validator + unified step screen) is the CORE defect fix. E1/E2/E3/E5/E6 are expansions licensed by SELECTIVE EXPANSION mode — accepted individually by the founder in the cherry-pick ceremony.

## Accepted Scope (in this plan)

Instruction truth layer:
- Compiler `src/lib/steps/compile.ts` + `classify.ts` + `validate.ts`; facts compiled post-generation in `applyTrustPipeline` (demo/localStorage/share plans get facts free); ConnectionsTable + CheckCard render from data. Pin references by SILKSCREEN LABEL only (pinLocationPhrase cut — vendor pin-order variance makes edge/ordinal phrases dangerous). Compiler failure is non-fatal: plan loads, visible banner, diagnostics entry.
- Single wire-color authority `src/lib/wire-colors.ts`; migration sweep across all six known call sites (harness.colorFor, connection-spars.colorForNet, step-media/diagrams.ts hex literals, wiringConnections/structuredNets fields, prose via validator).
